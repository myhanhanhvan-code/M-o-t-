A / AN

By First Sound

  • • an + vowel sound: an umbrella, an hour (silent h), an honest boy.
  • • a + consonant sound: a university (/juː/), a uniform, a European country.
THE

Specific & Unique

  • • Mentioned twice: I bought a coat. The coat is blue.
  • • Unique & Superlatives: the sun, the best, the first.
  • • Instruments & Places: play the piano, the cinema, the UK.
Ø (Zero Article)

No Article Needed

  • • Sports: play football, play tennis.
  • • Meals & Languages: have breakfast, speak English.
  • • General plurals & single mountains: Dogs are friendly, Mount Everest.
